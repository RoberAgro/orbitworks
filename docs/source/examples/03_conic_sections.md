## Goal

Visualize how eccentricity changes the polar orbit

$$
r(\theta)=\frac{p}{1+e\cos\theta}
$$

from a circle through ellipses and the escape conics.

## Planned experiment

The completed example will:

1. hold the semi-latus rectum $p$ fixed;
2. evaluate the orbit for representative eccentricities;
3. classify each trajectory as circular, elliptical, parabolic, or
   hyperbolic; and
4. compare their geometry in a common coordinate system.
