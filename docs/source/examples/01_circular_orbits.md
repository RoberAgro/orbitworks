## Goal

Calculate the circular orbital speed and period for a selected central body
and orbital radius, then verify that gravity supplies the required centripetal
acceleration.

## Planned experiment

The completed example will:

1. select a gravitational parameter $\mu$ and orbital radius $r$;
2. evaluate $v_c=\sqrt{\mu/r}$ and $T=2\pi\sqrt{r^3/\mu}$;
3. construct a circular trajectory; and
4. visualize the orbit and report its characteristic speed and period.
