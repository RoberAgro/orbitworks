## Goal

Compare circular and escape velocities at the same radius and use specific
mechanical energy to identify the boundary between bound and unbound motion.

## Planned experiment

The completed example will:

1. calculate $v_c=\sqrt{\mu/r}$ and $v_{\mathrm{esc}}=\sqrt{2\mu/r}$;
2. sample launch speeds around the escape threshold;
3. evaluate the corresponding specific mechanical energy; and
4. show how the sign of the energy separates bound and unbound trajectories.
